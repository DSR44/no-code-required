## Content Strategy & Creator Profile

## Unique Selling Point

**"A bodybuilder who never opened a terminal learned to build AI tools, automation pipelines, and mathematical engines — and she'll show you how she did it, step by step, without the jargon."**

What makes NCR different:
- **Real person, real story** — not a tech company's blog
- **Started from zero** — the creator genuinely didn't know what a terminal was
- **Practical, not theoretical** — every tool reviewed is actually used
- **Relatable tone** — feels like a friend explaining, not a professor lecturing
- **Visual learner friendly** — screenshots, step-by-step, no assumed knowledge

## Tone & Voice Guidelines

### DO:
- Write like you're talking to a friend at a coffee shop
- Use "I" and "you" — direct, personal
- Share half-formed thoughts ("I'm not sure about this but...")
- Use analogies from fitness, cooking, everyday life
- Be funny when it's natural — dry humor, not forced
- Admit when you don't know something
- Use short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max)
- Include personal anecdotes between information
- Make the reader feel like "if she can do it, I can too"

### DON'T:
- Sound like a LinkedIn post
- Use jargon without explaining it
- Be condescending or preachy
- Fake enthusiasm
- Write walls of text
- Assume the reader knows anything
- Use "thoughts?" or "what do you think?" engagement bait
- Sound like every other AI blog

### The "Coffee Shop Test":
Before publishing, read the post out loud. If it sounds like something you'd say to a friend over coffee, it's good. If it sounds like a textbook or a LinkedIn post, rewrite it.

---

## Post Structure Template

### Opening (2-3 paragraphs)
- Hook: a relatable moment, surprising fact, or personal anecdote
- Context: why this matters right now
- Promise: what the reader will learn/do

### Body (variable length)
- Short sections with clear headers (H2)
- Each section: explain → example → screenshot (if applicable)
- Personal anecdotes woven between sections (not forced)
- Tool links and recommendations where relevant
- No section longer than 300 words

### Closing (1-2 paragraphs)
- Summary or key takeaway
- Next step for the reader
- Optional: "here's what I'm working on next"

### Formatting:
- Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences)
- Bullet points for lists
- Bold for key terms
- Links to tools (outbound)
- No sidebar, no popups, no clutter

## SEO Approach

- Target long-tail keywords ("best AI tools for beginners 2026")
- Use questions as headers ("What is...?", "How do I...?")
- Internal linking between posts
- Meta descriptions that match the conversational tone
- Alt text on all images

---

## Affiliate Strategy

Only recommend tools you actually use. Period.

Categories with affiliate potential:
- AI writing tools (Jasper, Copy.ai, Writesonic)
- Image generators (Midjourney, DALL-E, Ideogram)
- Automation (n8n, Zapier, Make)
- Hosting (Vercel, Netlify, Cloudflare)
- Courses (Udemy, Coursera — only ones you've taken)

Rule: If you wouldn't recommend it to a friend, don't recommend it to your readers.
