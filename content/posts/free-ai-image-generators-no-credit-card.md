## How do you spot a "free" AI image generator that isn't?

Check for three tells before you waste an afternoon: credits, paid downloads, and the words "free trial."

Many tools advertising as "free AI photo generators" run a freemium model designed to hook you. They hand you 5–10 credits, then ask for a subscription. Here's what to look for:

* **Credits or tokens:** the most common trick. If a site mentions credits, your free run ends quickly.
* **The download button:** some sites let you generate for free but charge to download a high-resolution, watermark-free version. Generating is worthless if you can't save the file.
* **The fine print:** phrases like "free trial" or "limited free tier" are red flags. Genuinely free tools are upfront about their model, usually because they're supported by ads or backed by a larger ecosystem like Microsoft or Google.

The two tools above passed because they're transparent. They don't hide their limits behind confusing language.
