## What HeyGen's video translation actually does

Here's the process, stripped down:

1.  You upload a video (or use one you already made in HeyGen).
2.  You pick a target language from the **HeyGen language list**, which includes 175+ options.
3.  HeyGen translates the script, generates new voice audio, and re-syncs the speaker's lip movements to match the new language.
4.  You download the translated video. That's it.

The lip-sync is the part that matters most. Regular dubbing — even good dubbing — looks off because the mouth movements don't match the audio. HeyGen's AI reshapes the speaker's lips to fit the new language's phonemes. It's not perfect every time, but in a 60-second social clip, most viewers won't notice.

I tested it with a video I'd made in English and translated it into Spanish, Japanese, and Portuguese. The Spanish version looked natural enough that I would have believed it was filmed by a bilingual speaker. Japanese was slightly less convincing on certain consonant sounds, but still far better than subtitles alone.

## How to use the HeyGen language list to target the right audiences

The **HeyGen language list** is massive, but don't let that overwhelm you. Here's how I decide which languages to target:

1.  **Check your analytics.** Where are your current viewers coming from? If you see traffic from Germany, Brazil, or Japan, those are your first targets.
2.  **Research market size.** Languages like Spanish, Mandarin, and Arabic have huge numbers of native speakers. A single translation can open doors to millions.
3.  **Start with one or two.** Don't try to translate into 20 languages at once. Pick one or two high-opportunity languages, test the engagement, and then expand.

When you go to **translate languages** in HeyGen, you'll see the full list. I recommend starting with Spanish (spoken in 20+ countries), Portuguese (huge in Brazil), and French (spoken across multiple continents). These three alone can dramatically expand your reach.

The key is to treat translation not as an afterthought, but as a core part of your content strategy. Every video you create is a seed that can grow in multiple markets. HeyGen just makes planting those seeds incredibly easy.
