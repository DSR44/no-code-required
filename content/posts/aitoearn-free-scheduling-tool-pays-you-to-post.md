I've tested a lot of social media scheduling tools. Buffer, Hootsuite, Later, Publer — they all do roughly the same thing: you write posts, schedule them across platforms, and pay a monthly fee for the privilege. Then I found AiToEarn, and it broke the pattern completely. It's free, it's open-source, and it pays you to post.

That last part isn't a typo. AiToEarn has a built-in marketplace where brands post promotion tasks — and creators earn money per engagement. You schedule your content like normal, but some of those posts come with a paycheck attached. I've been using it for a few weeks, and here's what I found.

## What AiToEarn Actually Is

[AiToEarn](https://aitoearn.ai/en) is an open-source, AI-powered platform that handles the entire content lifecycle: creation, distribution, engagement, and monetization. It supports 14+ platforms including TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, X (Twitter), Facebook, Pinterest, LinkedIn, and several Chinese platforms like Douyin and Xiaohongshu.

The core promise: **Monetize · Publish · Engage · Create.**

Unlike [Buffer](https://buffer.com) or [Sprout Social](https://sproutsocial.com), AiToEarn isn't just a scheduler with a price tag. It's a marketplace. Brands post tasks (like "post about our product on TikTok"), creators accept tasks, post content, and earn money based on engagement. The payment models are:

- **CPS** (Cost Per Sale) — you earn when your post drives a sale
- **CPE** (Cost Per Engagement) — you earn per like, comment, save, or share
- **CPM** (Cost Per Mille) — you earn per 1,000 views

Some tasks pay $1 per post. Others pay $10 per 1,000 engagements. The top-tier tasks cap at $20,000 per post. That's not a typo.

## How the Scheduling Works

The scheduling itself is straightforward. You connect your social accounts, create posts in a calendar view, and schedule them across all platforms at once. One post, 14 platforms, one click.

What sets it apart from tools I covered in [the tools I actually use every day](/posts/the-tools-i-actually-use-every-day/) is the AI layer. AiToEarn doesn't just schedule — it helps you create:

- **AI content generation** — tell the Agent what you need, and it drafts posts, generates images (via Nano Banana), or creates videos (via Grok, Veo, Seedance)
- **AI reply management** — it monitors comments across platforms and generates contextual replies automatically
- **Comment mining** — it flags high-intent comments like "where's the link?" or "how do I buy this?" so you can respond fast
- **Brand monitoring** — tracks mentions of your brand across platforms

If you've read my guide on [automating client follow-ups with no code](/posts/automate-client-follow-ups-no-code/), the engagement automation here works on a similar principle — except it's built specifically for social media.

## The Monetization Marketplace

This is the part that makes AiToEarn genuinely different. Every other scheduler charges you. AiToEarn pays you.

Here's how it works:

1. **Browse tasks** — brands post promotion tasks with clear requirements (platform, content type, engagement targets)
2. **Accept a task** — some have follower minimums, most don't
3. **Create and post** — you make the content and publish it through AiToEarn
4. **Get paid** — earnings are calculated based on your chosen model (CPS, CPE, or CPM)

I tested this with a few low-cap tasks. A simple TikTok post earned me $5 in CPM. An Instagram story with a product link earned $10 in CPE. Not life-changing money of course, but these were posts I was going to make anyway — now they come with a paycheck.

The twist: you can also **post your own tasks**. Instead of paying Meta or Google for ads, you put a budget into AiToEarn and other creators promote your content. It's a peer-to-peer promotion network. If you've ever run [Facebook or Google ads](/posts/free-funnel-builders-compared/) and felt like you were burning money, this is a different model entirely.

## How to Get Started (3 Options)

AiToEarn gives you five ways to use it, but here are the three most relevant for no-code users:

### Option 1: Use the Website (Easiest)

Go to [aitoearn.ai](https://aitoearn.ai/en), create an account, and start using the web dashboard. No installation, no API keys, no setup. This is how I started.

### Option 2: Use It Inside OpenClaw

If you're already using [OpenClaw](https://openclaw.ai) (the AI agent platform), AiToEarn has a plugin. Install it with one command:

```
npx -y @aitoearn/openclaw-plugin-cli
```

Enter your API key (from the AiToEarn dashboard under Settings), and you can receive and complete tasks directly from your agent. This is the most hands-off approach.

### Option 3: Use It With Claude or Cursor

AiToEarn supports the MCP protocol, which means you can use it inside any AI tool that supports MCP — including Claude, Cursor, and other AI assistants. No installation needed, just configure the MCP connection.

## What I Like

**It's actually free.** Not "free trial" free. Not "free for 10 posts" free. The core scheduling and marketplace features are genuinely free. They take a cut from brand tasks, not from your pocket.

**Open-source.** The entire codebase is on [GitHub](https://github.com/yikart/AiToEarn). You can self-host it, audit it, or contribute to it. In a world where [every tool wants your data](/posts/the-privacy-problem-nobody-talks-about/), that matters.

**Multi-platform by default.** I schedule to TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, and X simultaneously. No extra fees per platform.

**The marketplace is real.** I was skeptical, but I completed tasks and got paid. The CPE model is particularly interesting — if your content gets good engagement, the earnings scale.

## What I Don't Like

**The UI is... functional.** It's not as polished as Buffer or Later. The design feels like a tool built by engineers, not designers. You'll get used to it, but don't expect a beautiful onboarding experience.

**Task availability varies.** Right now there are plenty of tasks, but some are sold out quickly. The best-paying tasks go fast.

**Chinese platform focus.** AiToEarn started in China, and some features (like the offline merchant promotion) are more relevant to Chinese markets. The international version works well, but you can tell the global audience is still growing.

**Documentation gaps.** Some features are only documented in Chinese. The English docs are improving but still catching up.
