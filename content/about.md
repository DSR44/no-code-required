## I didn't grow up with tech.

I'm a bodybuilder. I trained athletes. I ran programs. I knew how to push through physical pain and build discipline from scratch.

But code? Terminals? APIs? **I had no idea.**

When AI started getting real, I didn't understand what it even was. Not the concepts. Not the tools. Not the language. Everyone was talking about "machine learning" and "neural networks" like it was obvious — and I was sitting there thinking *"what is a terminal and why is everyone acting like I should know?"*

**But I started anyway.**

And here's what I learned: **you don't need a CS degree.** You don't need to "learn to code" in the traditional sense. You need to learn how machines think — how tools connect, how automation works, how to ask the right questions.

That's what this blog is. **The stuff I wish someone had shown me on day one.**

No jargon. No gatekeeping. No "just run this command" without explaining what it does.

## What you'll find here:

- **Tool reviews** — things I actually use, tested honestly
- **Tutorials** — step-by-step, with screenshots, for people who've never done this before
- **Automation workflows** — from "what is a webhook" to "my blog publishes itself"
- **Honest takes** — what works, what's hype, what I wish I'd known

## The tools I cover:

AI writing, image generation, no-code automation, APIs, GitHub, webhooks, content scheduling, video creation, and more.

**I'm not an expert.** I'm someone who started from zero and figured it out — and I'm bringing you along for the ride.
