Path A: Client Follow-Up Sequence

~90 min setup Make.com

80% of sales need at least 5 follow-ups — but most solo owners give up after one. This workflow sends 3 automatic follow-ups to every new lead. You write the emails once. The system runs forever.

The logic

Trigger: Someone fills out your contact form
Then: Wait 24h → Email 1 → Wait 3 days → Email 2 → Wait 4 days → Email 3 (final)

Steps 1–4: Set up the trigger

  1. Sign up at make.com (free). Click "Create a new scenario."
  2. Add module: Webhooks → Custom webhook. Make gives you a unique URL.
  3. Paste the webhook URL into your contact form (Typeform, Tally, Google Forms, or any form with webhook support).
  4. Submit a test entry with your own email so Make detects the data fields (name, email, etc.).
  5. Add Flow Control → Sleep → 24 hours. Gives them time to reply naturally first.
  6. Add Email module (Gmail, Outlook, or SMTP). Connect your sending account.
  7. Map the recipient field to the email from your webhook data. Map the name field for personalization.

Email 1 — Day 1 (after 24h wait)

Subject line
Following up on your inquiry
Body
Hey [name],

Just wanted to make sure my last message didn't get buried. Happy to answer any questions — just hit reply.

[your name]

Path A: Emails 2 & 3 + go live

Email 2 — Day 4 (3 days after Email 1)

Add another Sleep (3 days) module, then another Email module:

Subject line
Quick thought on [their problem]
Body
Hey [name],

I was thinking about [specific thing they mentioned]. Here's a quick tip: [one useful thing].

If you want to chat more, I'm around.

[your name]

Email 3 — Final check-in (4 days after Email 2)

Add Sleep (4 days), then final Email:

Subject line
Last note from me
Body
Hey [name],

I don't want to clog your inbox, so this is my last follow-up. If you'd like to pick things up later, just reply whenever works. No pressure.

[your name]

Test before you go live

  1. Set all Sleep timers to 1 minute for testing.
  2. Submit your own email through the form. Confirm all 3 emails arrive with correct names.
  3. Reset Sleep timers to real delays: 24 hours, 3 days, 4 days.
  4. Turn the scenario ON. Done — every new lead gets follow-ups automatically.
Full walkthrough with screenshots: www.nocoderequired.net/posts/automate-client-follow-ups-no-code/
Common mistakes: Don't automate before writing the emails. Don't skip the test run. Don't build a 7-step sequence on day one — ship the 3-email version first.

Path A: After it's running

What to do once it works

Option A — Add a 4th email at day 7. Share a case study, testimonial, or link to a relevant blog post. Value-add, no hard sell.

Option B — Log cold leads. Add a final Make step: if no reply after 7 days, create a Notion card with the lead's name, email, and what they asked about. Review once a week.

Make vs Zapier for this workflow

  • Make.com — better free tier (1,000 ops), more control over multi-step delays. Use for Path A.
  • Zapier — easier first experience, but 100 tasks/month caps out fast on sequences. Fine for Path B.

What this costs

  • Make.com free tier: $0/month (1,000 operations — enough for dozens of leads)
  • Form tool (Tally, Google Forms): $0
  • Your email (Gmail/Outlook): $0
  • Developer: not required

I built this in one afternoon. It landed 3 clients in the first month that I would have otherwise lost. The entire system costs $0 on Make's free tier. You don't need to be technical — you need 90 minutes and 3 short emails.

Paths B & C — Quick setup

Build Path A first. Come back to these when you want more automation wins.

Path B: Social Media Backup ~15 min · Zapier

  1. Sign up at zapier.com. Click "Create Zap."
  2. Trigger: Instagram → New Post on Your Account.
  3. Action: Google Sheets → Create Spreadsheet Row.
  4. Map fields: Caption → A, Image URL → B, Date → C, Post URL → D.
  5. Test with a recent post. Publish the Zap.

Why it matters: If your account gets suspended, you still have every caption, link, and date backed up — for free.

Path C: New Subscriber Alert ~10 min · Make.com

  1. In Make, create a new scenario.
  2. Trigger: Your email platform (Mailchimp, ConvertKit, Buttondown) → New Subscriber.
  3. Action: Telegram / Slack / Discord → Send Message.
  4. Message: "New subscriber: [email] — welcome them!"
  5. Turn it on. You get a ping every time someone joins your list.

Why it matters: Growth feels abstract until you get a real-time notification. It's motivating — and free.
